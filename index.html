On Windows, click the first download link at the top of the page. It'll save to your Downloads folder. Keep it for the next module." 16s

08 Back to the code.claude.com/docs/en/desktop-quickstart tab. "And that's it! No terminal, no code editor, nothing else. Just check you've got all five: the Claude Code setup file, Git downloaded or already on your Mac, your team Claude.ai login, your project folder, and access to the skills folder. Got them? See you in the next module!" 20s
